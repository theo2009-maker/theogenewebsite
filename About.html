About me

Who is Theogene?

Student, learner and builder from Rwanda.

My story

I am a student in Rwanda following a TVET Board RQF Level 5 course in Hobby Kernel Development. I am a beginner in systems programming, but I learn step by step and I practice every day.

I also care about my community. I take part in a water users' cooperative group, where I learn teamwork, responsibility and how technology can help people.

Work with me

RQF Level 5: Hobby Kernel Development (CSAHK501). The course uses a 336-page training manual about x86 operating system development in five Learning Outcomes.

Active member of a water users' cooperative group (Zone 2). I help with ideas, organization and teamwork.

Understand how a computer starts, write my own small kernel, and build professional websites and software for real people.
